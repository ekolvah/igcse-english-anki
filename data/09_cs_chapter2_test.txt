## 5 Computer Science 0478::02 Data transmission
wire | провод | a long thin piece of metal that carries electricity or signals | Serial transmission uses a single wire; parallel uses multiple wires. |
long-distance | на большое расстояние (дальний) | over a long way | Serial transmission is preferred for long-distance communication. |
sequentially | последовательно (по очереди) | one after another, in order | In serial transmission the bits are sent sequentially, one bit at a time. |
data skew | перекос данных (биты приходят не одновременно) | bits sent together on different wires arrive at slightly different times | Over long distances parallel transmission suffers from data skew. |
walkie-talkie | рация | a small radio for talking to another person; only one person can speak at a time | A walkie-talkie uses half-duplex transmission. |
sender | отправитель | the device or person that sends the data | The receiver sends a request back to the sender. |
receiver | получатель (приёмник) | the device that gets the data | The parity check is done at the receiver. |
noisy | с помехами (зашумлённый) | full of unwanted signals that can change the data | Errors may occur across noisy communication channels. |
communication channel | канал связи | the path that data travels along between two devices | Errors may occur across noisy communication channels. |
flip (a bit) | поменять бит (0 на 1 или 1 на 0); инвертировать бит | to change a 0 into a 1, or a 1 into a 0 | If two bits are flipped, the parity check will not find the error. |
corrupted | повреждённый (искажённый) | damaged, so that the data is wrong | The packet arrives corrupted at the receiving device. |
error-free | без ошибок | with no mistakes | The packet is sent again until it is received error-free. |
resend | отправить повторно (переслать) | to send again | The receiver asks the sender to resend the packet. |
request | запрос | a message that asks for something | The receiver sends a request back to the sender. |
data entry | ввод данных | typing data into a computer | A check digit finds mistakes made during data entry. |
transposed digits | переставленные цифры | two digits that have changed places, e.g. 21 instead of 12 | A check digit can find transposed or mistyped digits. |
identification number | идентификационный номер | a number that shows exactly which item or person it is | Check digits are added to identification numbers such as product barcodes. |

## 5 Computer Science 0478::05 The internet and its uses
Internet Service Provider (ISP) | интернет-провайдер (провайдер) | a company that gives people access to the internet for a fee | An ISP provides access to the internet and enforces its terms of service. |
fee | плата (за услугу) | money you pay for a service | The ISP provides internet access for a fee. |
terms of service | условия использования | rules you must agree to before you use a service | The ISP enforces its terms of service. |
enforce | следить за соблюдением (правил) | to make sure that people obey the rules | The ISP enforces its terms of service. |
host | размещать (сайт на сервере) | to keep a website on a server so that people can visit it | The web server hosts the company website. |
broadband | широкополосный интернет | a fast internet connection that is always on | A broadband router uses full-duplex transmission. |
download | скачать (загрузить) | to copy a file from the internet onto your computer | Users face security threats when downloading files. |
browse | просматривать (сайты) | to look at web pages on the internet | Users face security threats when browsing the internet. |
quarantine | поместить в карантин (изолировать файл) | to put a harmful file in a safe place where it cannot do damage | Anti-malware software can quarantine a virus. |
replicate | размножаться (копировать сам себя) | to make copies of itself | A virus replicates itself and damages files. |
halt | остановить (работу) | to make something stop | A virus can corrupt files or halt the system. |
unauthorised access | несанкционированный доступ | use of a computer or its data by someone who is not allowed to | A firewall protects the computer from unauthorised access. |
data theft | кража данных | stealing data | A firewall helps to stop outgoing data theft. |
login credentials | данные для входа (логин и пароль) | the username and password you type to get into an account | Online banking websites use HTTPS to send login credentials. |
online banking | интернет-банк (онлайн-банкинг) | using your bank account through a website or an app | Online banking websites use HTTPS. |
intercept | перехватить | to catch data while it is being sent | HTTP sends data in plain text that can be intercepted. |
network traffic | сетевой трафик | all the data moving into and out of a network | A firewall monitors inbound and outbound network traffic. |
inbound | входящий | coming into a computer or a network | A firewall checks inbound traffic. |
outbound | исходящий | going out of a computer or a network | A firewall checks outbound traffic. |
