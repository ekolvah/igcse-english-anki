greatest | наибольший, самый большой | the biggest | Find the greatest number of boxes she can buy. |
identical | одинаковые (совершенно одинаковые) | exactly the same | Two identical cubes are joined together. |

satisfy | удовлетворять (подходить под условие) | make an equation or inequality true | x = 3 satisfies x + 2 > 4. |

## 2 Maths 0580::01 Number
accuracy | точность (близость к истинному значению) | how close a value or a measurement is to the exact (true) value | Give your answer to an appropriate degree of accuracy. |
approximation | приближение, приближённое значение | a value close to the exact value, but not exact | 3.14 is an approximation of π. |
directed numbers | числа со знаком (положительные и отрицательные) | numbers with a + or − sign | 3 °C − 8 °C = −5 °C |
order of operations | порядок действий | which part of a calculation to do first: brackets, indices, × and ÷, then + and − | 2 + 3 × 4 = 14, not 20 |
brackets | скобки | the symbols ( ) that show what to work out first | (2 + 3) × 4 = 20 |
sum | сумма | the answer when you add numbers | The sum of 3 and 4 is 7. |
product | произведение | the answer when you multiply numbers | The product of 3 and 4 is 12. |
hundredths | сотые (доли) | the second place after the decimal point | 0.07 = 7 hundredths |
triangle numbers | треугольные числа | numbers that make triangles of dots: 1, 3, 6, 10, … | 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21 |
lowest terms | несократимый вид (дробь, которую нельзя сократить) | a fraction that cannot be simplified any more | 6/8 in its lowest terms is 3/4. |
vulgar fraction | обыкновенная дробь (в этом учебнике = неправильная дробь) | a fraction written as a/b; the Hodder book uses it for an improper fraction | Change the vulgar fraction 27/4 into a mixed number. → 6¾ |
zero index | нулевой показатель степени | the power 0; any number to the power 0 is 1 | 5⁰ = 1 |
negative index | отрицательный показатель степени | a power with a minus sign; it means 1 divided by the number | 2⁻³ = 1/2³ = 1/8 |
fractional index | дробный показатель степени | a power that is a fraction; it means a root | 8^⅓ = ∛8 = 2 |
laws of indices | свойства степеней | the rules for multiplying, dividing and raising powers | aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ, aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ |
multiplier | процентный множитель (на что умножить) | the number you multiply by to increase or decrease by a percentage | An increase of 5% is a multiplier of 1.05. |
exponential growth | экспоненциальный рост | increasing by the same percentage each time | $1000 grows by 5% each year: 1000 × 1.05ⁿ |
exponential decay | экспоненциальное убывание | decreasing by the same percentage each time | A car loses 15% of its value each year: V × 0.85ⁿ |
principal | исходная сумма, вклад (на которую начисляют проценты) | the money you put in or borrow at the start | In I = PRT/100, P is the principal. |
cost price | цена покупки (себестоимость) | the price paid to buy or make something | Cost price $40, selling price $50 → profit $10 |
selling price | цена продажи | the price something is sold for | Profit = selling price − cost price |
discount | скидка | money taken off the price | A 10% discount on $50 is $5. |
gross pay (gross earnings) | зарплата до вычетов (начисленная) | pay before tax and other deductions | Gross pay = basic pay + overtime + bonus |
net pay | зарплата на руки (после вычетов) | pay after deductions such as tax and insurance | Net pay = gross pay − deductions |
deductions | вычеты, удержания (налог, страховка) | money taken from pay, e.g. tax and insurance | Gross pay $188.25, deductions $33.43 → net pay $154.82 |
basic pay | основная (базовая) оплата | normal pay for the normal working hours, without extras | basic pay of $12 per hour for 38 hours |
overtime | сверхурочная работа (сверхурочные) | hours worked in addition to the normal working week | She works 6 hours of overtime. |
time and a half | полуторная оплата (×1.5) | overtime pay of 1.5 times the basic hourly rate | $12 per hour → $18 per hour at time and a half |
double time | двойная оплата (×2) | overtime pay of twice the basic hourly rate | $12 per hour → $24 per hour at double time |
piece work | сдельная оплата (за каждую вещь) | being paid for each item made, not for the hours worked | $0.50 for each shirt made |
bonus | премия, бонус | an extra payment added to basic pay | basic pay $300 + bonus $18 |
compound measures | составные величины (скорость, плотность…) | a measure made from two other measures | speed in km/h, density in g/cm³ |
average speed | средняя скорость | total distance ÷ total time | 150 km in 2 hours → 75 km/h |
metric units | метрические единицы | units based on tens: mm, cm, m, km, g, kg, ml, l | 1 km = 1000 m, 1 kg = 1000 g |
unitary method | метод приведения к единице (сначала цена одной штуки) | first find the value of one, then multiply | 5 pens cost $4 → 1 pen $0.80 → 8 pens $6.40 |
common ratio | знаменатель геометрической прогрессии | the number you multiply by to get the next term of a geometric sequence | 2, 6, 18, 54, …: common ratio = 3 |
proper subset | собственное подмножество | a subset that is not the whole set; symbol ⊂ | {1, 2} ⊂ {1, 2, 3} |

## 2 Maths 0580::02 Algebra
constant of proportionality | коэффициент пропорциональности | the number k in y = kx or y = k/x | y ∝ x, y = 10 when x = 2 → k = 5 |
equation of a straight line | уравнение прямой | y = mx + c, where m is the gradient and c is the y-intercept | y = 2x + 3 |
elimination method | метод сложения, метод исключения | solve simultaneous equations by adding or subtracting them to remove one letter | (x + y = 10) − (x − y = 2) → 2y = 8 |
substitution method | метод подстановки | solve simultaneous equations by putting one equation into the other | y = 2x, x + y = 9 → x + 2x = 9 |
polynomial | многочлен (полином) | an expression with several terms of whole-number powers of x | 3x³ − 2x + 5 |
gradient function | производная функция (формула градиента) | a formula for the gradient of a curve at any point: dy/dx | y = x² → gradient function 2x |
second derivative | вторая производная | the result of differentiating twice: d²y/dx² | y = x³ → dy/dx = 3x², d²y/dx² = 6x |
point of inflexion | точка перегиба | a point where the curve changes the way it bends | y = x³ has a point of inflexion at (0, 0). |

## 2 Maths 0580::03 Geometry
acute-angled triangle | остроугольный треугольник | all three angles are less than 90° | angles 50°, 60°, 70° |
obtuse-angled triangle | тупоугольный треугольник | one angle is more than 90° | angles 120°, 40°, 20° |
side–side–side (SSS) | по трём сторонам (признак равенства треугольников) | two triangles are congruent if all three sides are equal | |
side–angle–side (SAS) | по двум сторонам и углу между ними (признак равенства) | two sides and the angle between them are equal, so the triangles are congruent | |
angle–side–angle (ASA) | по стороне и двум прилежащим углам (признак равенства) | two angles and the side between them are equal, so the triangles are congruent | |
right angle–hypotenuse–side (RHS) | по гипотенузе и катету (признак равенства прямоугольных треугольников) | right angle, hypotenuse and one other side are equal, so the triangles are congruent | |
area factor | коэффициент площадей (k²) | for similar shapes: the scale factor squared | scale factor 3 → area factor 9 |
volume factor | коэффициент объёмов (k³) | for similar solids: the scale factor cubed | scale factor 2 → volume factor 8 |
angle at the centre | центральный угол (угол в центре) | the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference on the same arc | angle at circumference 40° → angle at centre 80° |
angles in the same segment | вписанные углы на одну дугу (равны) | angles at the circumference standing on the same chord are equal | |
tangents from an external point | касательные из внешней точки (равны по длине) | two tangents from one point outside a circle are the same length | |
